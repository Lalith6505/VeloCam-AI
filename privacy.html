Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Core Philosophy: 100% Offline Edge Architecture

VeloCam AI is engineered as an entirely self-contained automotive computer vision and telemetry application. We believe that your driving routes, video footage, driving behavior, and vehicle telemetry are strictly personal. VeloCam AI does not transmit, store, or forward any data to remote servers, external APIs, cloud storage providers, or third-party analytics platforms.

2. Camera & Sensor Data Processing

The application requires runtime access to:

  • Camera Hardware: Utilized strictly for real-time lane detection, vehicle tracking, and optional local loop dashcam recording. Image frames are processed in-memory directly on the device's NPU/GPU and discarded immediately after inference unless explicitly saved to your local incident vault.
  • Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU): The 3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope sensors measure vehicle dynamics, vibration, and collision forces locally.
  • Fine & Coarse Location (GPS): Used solely to compute ground speed and optionally append GPS coordinates to local telemetry CSV files and HUD overlays. GPS data is never transmitted externally.
  • Audio Microphone: Optionally records cockpit audio during loop recording. Audio processing is completely local.

3. Zero Ads, Zero Trackers, Zero Third-Party SDKs

VeloCam AI contains zero advertising frameworks, zero behavioral tracking software, zero third-party diagnostic SDKs, and zero social media integration code. The application does not track user behavior or create diagnostic fingerprints.

4. Data Storage & User Ownership

All recorded video files, incident logs, and CSV telemetry datasets are stored inside the application's sandboxed local storage on your Android device. You maintain absolute ownership of all files and can delete, export, or share them at any time using standard Android system intents.

5. Policy Inquiries

For any inquiries regarding this policy or technical architecture, you may inspect the open source documentation repository on GitHub or contact the developer directly via the Google Play Store developer portal.
